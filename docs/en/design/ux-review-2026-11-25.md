# UI/UX review of the PowerPoint add-in – all elements, combinations and workflows (2026-11-25)

German version: [../../de/design/ux-review-2026-11-25.md](../../de/design/ux-review-2026-11-25.md). The rules that come out
of it are in [interaction-ux.md](interaction-ux.md).

**Why.** The maintainer: "Do the UI/UX review for all elements, combinations and workflows – top priority." Earlier the
same day: "the overlay bar and the pie menu are a catastrophe, they explain nothing", "still the same", and it must be
"powerful, but understandable for new users and older people, not too much text in the overlay".

## 1. Method

The review looks at what the user sees, not at the code:

- **Harness** `tests/powerpoint-com/scenarios/ux-audit.ps1` in real PowerPoint: for every library category (113
  categories, 1405 items) one or two items of different types are inserted on their own slide, selected and
  photographed with their bar and handles; the pie menu of the first item of each category is photographed too. Ten
  combinations (shaft + gear + bearing, gear pair, PV string + cable, pump, flowchart, chart part, chart limit lines,
  table, wall + door, resistor), a multi-selection and two workflows (insert box, insert at the pointer near a shaft)
  follow. Every case writes its bar ids, handles, shown handles, all command ids and pie entries to `audit.jsonl`.
- **Before**: the build after the first overlay round (handle layout, eight pie places), run 2026-11-25 16:09.
- **After**: the build with the second round (progressive disclosure, four-button bar) and the fixes of this review.
- Contact sheets of all pictures are reviewed category by category (`local-resources/powerpoint-tests/…/ux-audit`).

## 2. Numbers (before)

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Elements photographed | 211 (one or two per category) |
| Buttons in the bar | average 8.1, maximum 16 (fluid 4/2-way valve), 63 bars with more than 9 |
| Rotate 90° in the bar | 197 of 211 – also for round, symmetric symbols where it changes nothing visible |
| Mirror in the bar | 133 (horizontal) / 143 (vertical) – often without a visible effect |
| "Insert" as the first bar button | 211 of 211 – also for a text, a label, a hatch or a map frame |
| Handles of InventaSketch (besides PowerPoint's) | average 2.1, maximum 12 |
| Pie places greyed out | average 1.8 of 8 |

**After** (run 2026-11-25 16:39, 232 records): buttons in the bar average 4.7, maximum 5 (four plus "⋯"), none above;
InventaSketch handles shown right after selection average 1.4 (the rest on approach); every bar label readable; the pie
names the element in its centre.

## 3. Findings

Severity: **A** blocks understanding or use, **B** slows down or confuses, **C** polish.

### 3.1 Everywhere

| # | Finding | Sev. | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **The bar looks inactive.** It fades with the pointer distance (fully visible only within 12 px, gone after ~190 px); most pictures show a pale, washed-out bar. Text buttons use 9.5 px bold text – unreadable at 100 % and for older users. | A | Fully visible within 90 px, fading over 420 px; labels 12 px semibold, pictogram labels 12.5 px, tooltips 11.5 px, dropdown rows 32 px with 12 px text. *(done in this change)* |
| G2 | **Too many buttons, no main action.** 8 on average, up to 16; "Insert" first on every element although it is the main action for few; four line dropdowns that all show "—". | A | At most four buttons plus "⋯": the element's main action with a short label, three pictograms, "⋯" opens the pie. Rotate/mirror, duplicate/delete go to the pie. *(round 2)* |
| G3 | **Handles crowd the element.** Up to 12 InventaSketch handles plus PowerPoint's eight; four "+" around a gear, a "+" with a dashed lead line far from its port, blue "i"-like corner dots on every segment of a polyline or route, triangles on three sides of flowchart boxes – the top one under PowerPoint's rotate handle. | A | Calm by default: right after selection only the frame, the point diamonds of lines and **one** primary "+"; everything else fades in when the pointer comes near (Shift shows all); lock badges are quiet grey dots until hovered. *(round 2)* |
| G4 | **The pie says nothing about the element.** All 211 pies look the same (Text, Edit ▸, Insert, Duplicate, Style ▸, Delete, View ▸, Arrange ▸); the element's own commands are one level deeper under "Edit ▸"; labels 9 px. | A | Keep the eight fixed places (learnable), but the centre names the element and the Edit place carries the element's name ("Gear ▸") and opens its commands on hover; labels 12 px, ring larger. *(centre: round 2; size and text: this change; hover-open and element name on the Edit place: follow-up F1)* |
| G5 | **Small symbols disappear under PowerPoint's handles.** Fluid, electrical, kinematic and plan symbols insert at their standard size of 5–12 mm; at 100 % zoom PowerPoint's eight handles and the rotate handle cover them (fluid gauge, check valve, revolute joint, actuation, tank). | B | A slide-wide symbol size (like the line group: "symbols ×1 / ×1.5 / ×2") so schematics stay consistent instead of scaling single symbols; the default for new slides ×1.5. *(follow-up F2)* |
| G6 | **Placement preview lies.** *(Correction after the diagnosis run: the parts that seemed to land in the top-left corner in the combination pictures came from invalid item ids in the harness – `mech.shaft` is a type, the items are `mech.shaft.body`/`mech.shaft.line`; a gear and a bearing inserted from a selected shaft or gear land on the shaft axis. The preview finding below stands.)* Insert at the pointer shows the item in its default view and size (a bearing looks like a piece of shaft) and only on the click it becomes the fitted part – or not at all, when the selected unit's first object is not the shaft. | A | The ghost shows the fitted result near a shaft ("Fits here – click to insert"), insert tries every object of the selection and the shaft a selected gear sits on. *(round 2 + engine agent: fit to the shaft diameter in every view)* |
| G7 | **Parts do not fit the shaft and distort.** Bearings, gears, wheels keep their own bore; scaling makes balls ellipses. | A | Bore = shaft diameter on insert and on snapping, in every view; round features stay round under any scaling. *(engine agent, in progress)* |
| G8 | **Ambiguous pictograms.** "+"/"−" for points, grid, panels, planets …; the same list pictogram for "data", "table" and "legend"; view pictograms that look alike (stick vs. section). | B | One pictogram one meaning, an engine test that fails on reuse with different meanings; "+"/"−" only with a label. *(follow-up F3)* |
| G9 | **Cryptic labels.** "F = 0", "Seite wechseln", "Zur anderen Seite bie…", "Wegpunkte zeigen" as tiny text buttons. | B | Labels are verbs with an object and fit (no ellipsis in the bar); technical abbreviations only with a tooltip that explains them ("Degrees of freedom F = 0"). *(follow-up F3)* |

### 3.2 Element families

| Family | What the pictures show | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Basic shapes, flowchart | Three quick-add triangles, the top one under the rotate handle; bar starts with Insert. | One triangle (bottom) until the pointer comes near; main action "Text". *(round 2)* |
| Lines, arrows, dimensions, leaders | Four look-alike dropdowns; dimension and leader insert tiny (text 2 mm). | Main action "Line ▾" (type), heads and style as two telling dropdowns; minimum insert size of annotations 25 mm. *(round 2 / F2)* |
| Gears, shafts, bearings, couplings | Four "+" plus a "+" with a long lead line; the stick view "+" sits at the frame corner. | One "+" at the free side, others on approach; fitted insert with ghost; fit to the shaft. *(round 2 / engine)* |
| Planetary sets, ring gears | Up to five "+" ("Insert planet here") at once. | Only on approach. *(round 2)* |
| Linkages (kinematics) | 14 buttons (F = n, coupler curve, ghosts, crossed …); joints tiny. | Main action "Move" (motion), rest in Edit; symbol size F2. |
| Electrical, fluid, process symbols | Correct ports, but symbols tiny; bars with 6–8 dropdowns (valve: 16 buttons). | Four-button bar; symbol size F2. |
| Cables, pipes, roads, routes | Corner dots look like "i"; two "+" per free end before round 1. | Corner dots on approach only, as plain small dots. *(round 2)* |
| Charts | Bar of 14, faint; grips always visible. | Main action "Data"; grips near the lines or for the selected part. *(round 2)* |
| Tables | Four side "+" at once. | One "+" (bottom) until the pointer is near a side. *(round 2)* |
| Maps | "+"/"−" without labels, "Wegpunkte zeigen". | Labelled actions (F3). |
| Robots | 14 buttons. | Main action "Pose", rest in Edit. |

| Case | Finding | Decision |

## 4. What changes, in which order

1. **Done before this review**: no overlapping handles, one "+" meaning, eight labelled pie places, insert box closes on
   outside click, table "+" on every side, chart grips (round 1).
2. **Round 2 (merged)**: progressive disclosure, four-button bar with a labelled main action, pie centre with the element
   name, fitted insert with a truthful ghost.
3. **This change**: readable bar and pie (text sizes, larger ring), gentler fading.
4. **Done since**: engine fit to the shaft diameter in every view, round features stay round (G7); the fitted ghost
   (`Fitting.PlaceNear`) and the clicked side for attached parts; the selection halo and "Select connected" (nets,
   what moves along, following elements, loops, room walls).
5. **Follow-ups** (ROADMAP, COM first): F1 element name on the Edit place with hover-open; F2 slide-wide symbol size (done 2026-11-25: More ▸ Symbol size ×1/×1.5/×2, scenario `symbol-size`);
   F3 pictogram and label hygiene with an engine test; F4 multi-selection review; then the same rules in the web add-in
   and LibreOffice.

## 5. Verification

The "after" run of the same harness is compared picture by picture with the "before" run; acceptance: no bar with more
than four buttons plus "⋯"; at most one "+" and no lock badge visible right after selection; every label readable at
100 % zoom; the fitted insert ghost equals the inserted result; a bearing on a shaft has the shaft's bore in every view.
